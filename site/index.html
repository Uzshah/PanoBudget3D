About the scene and budgets

Current C6-PI-SR scene, trained for 29k chart updates plus 1k ERP refinement. Full mode preserves degree-three spherical harmonics. Reduced budgets use Spark's view-dependent LoD renderer; they are a delivery preview, not the paper's learned C5 gating results. The initial path uses training-camera positions.

Renderer: Spark 2.3.0 with Three.js 0.180.0. Replica research asset. Browser FPS is measured locally and is not the paper's CUDA speed benchmark.
